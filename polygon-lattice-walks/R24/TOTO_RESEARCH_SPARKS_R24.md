## Model and exact covariance
For fixed unknown x in R^2, known anchor a_i, observe squared range d_i=||x-a_i||^2+e_i. Errors have mean zero, common finite variance sigma^2 and are independent across readings; no distributional shape assumed. Subtract known ||a_i||^2 and fit a linear regression with a free intercept (relaxing the physical intercept ||x||^2). Center within each group g to eliminate its intercept. Let S_g=sum_i (a_i-mean_g)(a_i-mean_g)^T. Ordinary least squares on the centered observations with common slope -2x gives unbiased estimate xhat. The noise covariance after centering is sigma^2 times the centering projector; since the centered design columns are in that projector's range, the covariance is exactly sigma^2/4 times (sum_g S_g)^(-1) when the sum is nonsingular. This follows by multiplying the linear estimator by its transpose, not by a nonlinear Fisher-information claim. Groupwise centering permits independent unknown additive offsets per group. If no offsets exist, pooling and centering all raw observations may improve the design by adding between-group scatter; separate centering is not claimed optimal for the physical nonlinear model.
The sum is positive definite iff no nonzero vector is simultaneously in every S_g kernel: v^T S_g v is a sum of squares. Thus even rank-one groups can complement each other. Independent readings are essential; reusing one anchor's noisy reading is not an independent new datum.

## Explicit pair of weak groups
For h>0 use groups {(-1,0),(1,0),(0,h)} and {(0,-1),(0,1),(h,0)}. Their centered matrices are diag(2,2h^2/3) and diag(2h^2/3,2). Each worst directional variance divided by sigma^2 is 3/(8h^2) for 0<h<=1/2. Combined scatter is (2+2h^2/3) I, giving worst directional variance divided by sigma^2 = 1/(8+8h^2/3). As h tends to zero each separate estimate becomes arbitrarily ill conditioned but combined variance tends to sigma^2/8. At h=0 the individual two-dimensional inverses do not exist, yet the combined inverse does. Six independent readings in a common calibrated coordinate frame are assumed. These isosceles reference groups are not R22's unique scalene anchor-recognition construction; no claim they can be discovered from unlabeled distance data.

## Area identity and fixed trace budget
For a single group of n anchors, form the n-by-3 matrix M whose rows are (1,a_ix,a_iy). Column operations center the last two columns without changing det(M^T M). Then M^T M becomes block diagonal with blocks n and S. Cauchy-Binet gives n det S=sum over unordered triples det(M_triple)^2=4 sum over unordered triples Area(triple)^2. This is a standard determinant identity specialized to planar anchors. The trace T=sum ||a_i-mean||^2 is a second-moment budget. For any positive definite planar total scatter S with eigenvalues l1,l2, l1+l2=T, min(l1,l2)<=T/2 and l1*l2<=T^2/4. Thus worst directional variance >=sigma^2/(2T), total mean squared position error =sigma^2 T/(4 det S)>=sigma^2/T. Both are equalities iff S=(T/2)I. This is exact optimality among centered linear designs with fixed scatter trace and the stated noise model; not optimality for a fixed number of anchors, travel cost, real range noise, anchor recognition, or nonlinear estimators. Different groups contribute their within-group scatter; the single-group area identity must not be applied to their pooled coordinates without accounting for between-group scatter.
Prior art: Bishop et al., Optimality Analysis of Sensor-Target Localization Geometries, https://www.csc.kth.se/~adrianb/docs/bishopGeometry2008.pdf ; Optimal Ground-Based Anchor Placements for Least-Squares Multilateration, https://doi.org/10.1049/ell2.70344 ; Geometry-Aware Set-Membership Multilateration: Directional Bounds and Anchor Selection, https://borowiecki.dev/pdf/2603.14263 (search excerpts checked; the last explicitly uses centered scatter for different bounded-noise criteria). ## Shared-reading warning
For anchors (0,0),(1,0),(0,1) the two contrast errors are (e0-e1)/2 and (e0-e2)/2. Their covariance divided by sigma^2 is [[1/2,1/4],[1/4,1/2]], not diagonal. Treating those two contrasts as independent would understate worst-direction variance (true largest eigenvalue 3/4 instead of 1/2 in normalized units). Do not count the same raw reading as independent twice.
