# Polygon lattice walks | مشي الشبكات متعددة الأضلاع

Independent research lane for random walks generated by regular polygon directions, their algebraic difference lattices, collision observables and scale-phase profiles. This lane is mathematically separate from Pascal's triangle and the Singmaster conjecture.

The records below were moved as complete source/script/stdout triples from the historical path `pascal-singmaster/toto-sparks/R09`–`R19`. Thirty-one of the 33 files are byte-identical to their source blobs. Two prose-only corrections repair the R09 relative link and the R18 round heading; their original versions remain in Git history. The old index now points here. Earlier rounds R01–R08 remain in the older folder because they study moment identification and discrete observation rather than polygon lattice walks.

## Current position

R18 closes one specific open item in R16: the dodecagonal phase profile varies. R19 adds a separate inverse-identification result from radial moments. Neither establishes physical interpretation or novelty; R18 does not establish minimal period or global extrema, and R19 does not identify orientation or cover noisy/arbitrary competitors. R18 was reviewed by an internal analytical collaboration agent in this workflow, not a standalone ChatGPT conversation; no provider session ID exists. This is model-side analysis, not human peer review. See [review provenance](R18/REVIEW_PROVENANCE.md) and the [retrospective dependency audit](DEPENDENCY_AUDIT.md).

The remaining active questions include independent review of the local-limit transfer in R16/R18, literature comparison for the model-specific phase statement, period minimality, noise stability of R19's inverse procedure, and higher-cumulant observables for the broader inverse-identification question.

## Reproduce


Use the corresponding `Rxx/probe.py` and `Rxx/probe.log` for other rounds. The records are session-derived and non-canonical; a successful certificate does not imply human peer review or a physical result.
