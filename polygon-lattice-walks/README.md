# Polygon lattice walks | مشي الشبكات متعددة الأضلاع

Independent research lane for random walks generated by regular polygon directions, their algebraic difference lattices, collision observables and scale-phase profiles. This lane is mathematically separate from Pascal's triangle and the Singmaster conjecture.

The records below were moved as complete source/script/stdout triples from the historical path `pascal-singmaster/toto-sparks/R09`–`R19`. File contents were preserved; the old index now points here. Earlier rounds R01–R08 remain in the older folder because they study moment identification and discrete observation rather than polygon lattice walks.

## Current position

R18 closes one specific open item in R16: the dodecagonal phase profile varies. R19 adds a separate inverse-identification result from radial moments. Neither establishes physical interpretation or novelty; R18 does not establish minimal period or global extrema, and R19 does not identify orientation or cover noisy/arbitrary competitors. HORUS reviewed the R18 algebra and certificate in-session; that is model-side review, not human peer review.

The remaining active questions include independent review of the local-limit transfer in R16/R18, literature comparison for the model-specific phase statement, period minimality, noise stability of R19's inverse procedure, and higher-cumulant observables for the broader inverse-identification question.

## Reproduce

Each round keeps its own derivation, executable certificate and actual stdout. From the repository root run:

```sh
python3 -I polygon-lattice-walks/R18/probe.py
```

Use the corresponding `Rxx/probe.py` and `Rxx/probe.log` for other rounds. The records are session-derived and non-canonical; a successful certificate does not imply human peer review or a physical result.
