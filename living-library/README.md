## How to use it

Read the claim with its scope, method, evidence, limitation and class. Follow the certificate and recorded stdout. Follow corrections without deleting the original failure. Record who proposed the direction, who derived the result and who verified it. Add independent records under unique round paths, then link them here or at the corresponding lane index. A merge archives work; it does not establish truth.

**Next priorities:** independent reproduction of octagon interior counts/tail proof; recover missing original scripts or label newly written replacements as new evidence; connect the fuller living-reference result/correction register to public stable records. This initial index is not yet a complete export or an automatically maintained catalog. No originality or human peer-review claim.
