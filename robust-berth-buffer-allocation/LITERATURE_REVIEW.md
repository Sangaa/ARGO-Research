# Preliminary literature review | R01

Search date: 2026-11-27. Scope: robust berth scheduling, time-buffer allocation and the recurrence used for single-server delay propagation. This is a focused preliminary search, not an exhaustive survey.

## Relevant established work

- Xu, Chen & Quan (2012), “Robust berth scheduling with uncertain vessel delay and handling time,” studies buffers between vessels in berth scheduling under uncertainty.
- Wu & Miao (2021), “An Efficient Procedure for Inserting Buffers to Generate Robust Berth Plans in Container Terminals,” addresses buffer insertion into predefined berth plans.
- Briskorn, Leung & Pinedo (2011), “Robust scheduling on a single machine using time buffers,” studies robust single-machine schedules and time buffers.
- Lindley (1952), “The theory of queues with a single server,” is classical background for the max recurrence underlying workload propagation.
- Wu, Zeng & D’Ariano (published online 2026), “Robust berth allocation and scheduling under uncertain arrival and processing times,” jointly optimizes berth allocation and vessel sequence under uncertainty.

These works establish that robust berth planning, time buffers, and single-server recurrences are not new topics. R01 deliberately fixes a single berth and a fixed order, then formulates the minimum sum of idle gaps needed to enforce a common worst-case delay cap under independent rectangular bounded lateness and overrun. The search did not establish priority or absence for that exact specialization. A search gap is not evidence of originality.
