## 1. Guardrail: a platform's listed drivers are not a public vector of prediction weights

Three different objects must remain separate:
(a) macro regression estimates or historical contribution shares (WGC GRAM), which vary by time and estimation;
(b) technical indicator **aggregation weights** (TradingView), e.g., arithmetic mean of composite groups, which are *not* economic price-response coefficients;
(c) human analyst scenario emphasis or forecasts (LBMA, Goldman, JPMorgan, local news), generally not accompanied by reproducible 3-session numerical contributions.

**DO NOT** infer "Goldman assigns central banks 25%" or "GRAM assigns DXY 30%" from a narrative, nor compare a 2027 price target directly with our XAU h=3 forecast. Methodological transparency is an explicit comparison dimension.

## 4. NEW research challenger **M2-SHADOW-v0.1**: weights proposed on 2026-11-24; NOT fitted and NOT active

**Design rationale.** WGC GRAM shows risk & uncertainty as a historically relevant dimension absent from M1; ETF data is weekly/monthly and can lag a 3-session horizon; 5-session MOM is a direct short-horizon observation; REAL/DXY may encode overlapping monetary signals; none of these gives an empirically measured weight. These motivate a **controlled alternative to falsify**, not a proven improvement.

| Feature score | Frozen M1 | Proposed M2 shadow | Delta | Proposed reason / key uncertainty |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| REAL (10Y TIPS yield five-session bp change, sign inverse, scale 20bp) | 0.35 | 0.30 | -0.05 | maintain opportunity cost, reserve weight for separate risk signal |
| DXY (5-session % change, sign inverse, scale 1.5%) | 0.30 | 0.20 | -0.10 | lower assumed marginal contribution if correlated with REAL; not yet empirically proven |
| MOM (XAU 5-session % change, scale 4%) | 0.20 | 0.25 | +0.05 | short horizon relevance; may fail in reversal/liquidation |
| ETF (latest publicly released global physical ETF weekly net tonnes, scale 40 tonnes) | 0.15 | 0.10 | -0.05 | weekly reporting lag; not evidence of weak physical demand |
| RISK (Cboe VIX 5 **completed, released** session percentage change, scale 20%) | 0.00 | 0.15 | +0.15 | exploratory risk/uncertainty proxy; sign can invert when margin calls force gold liquidation |
| **Total** | **1.00** | **1.00** | **0.00** | all candidate weights heuristic, not market truth |

**Risk score**: \`s_RISK = clip(((VIX_t/VIX_t-5)-1)/0.20, -1, +1)\`; preliminary positive sign hypothesizes risk-off gold support; Cboe VIX is ~30-day **US equity implied volatility**, not a gold-specific safe-haven coefficient. Source https://www.cboe.com/insights/posts/what-the-vix-and-vix-1-d-indices-attempt-to-measure-and-how-they-differ and https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/VIXCLS/ . Report that forced liquidations/cash-squeeze regimes can reverse this sign; never silently switch sign after outcomes.

\`S_M2 = 0.30*s_REAL + 0.20*s_DXY + 0.25*s_MOM + 0.10*s_ETF + 0.15*s_RISK\`
\`forecast_M2_XAU(h) = XAU_last_completed_close * exp(0.013*sqrt(h/3)*S_M2)\`, h=3 is research target; amplitude kept identical to M1 to isolate feature-score change. No numerical forecast may be labelled issued until exact sources, as-of, slot and signed/immutable record are frozen before target.

## 5. Egypt-specific update: first repair the *measurement*, not the predictive weights

\`reference_metal_21 = (XAU_USD/31.1034768) * 0.875 * FX_USD_EGP\`.
For an **observed same-time local dealer ask** with recognized purity/source:
\`q_ask = ask_dealer_21/reference_metal_21 - 1\`.
Store separate \`bid_dealer_21\`, spread = ask - bid, and labelled making charge / coin minting charge; do not count spread twice when q is ask-derived.

**Source classes**:
- \`DERIVED_REFERENCE\`: formula/aggregator/computed quote, e.g. EgyptGoldPrice.com; cannot be claimed an observed transaction premium.
- \`MULTI_VENDOR_AGGREGATED\`: median platform, e.g. 3omlat; may be useful as liquidity/dispersion observation, but not the exact dealer price.
- \`VENDOR_PUBLISHED_ASK_BID\`: published comparable actual seller quotes if independently verifiable and both terms/time visible.
- \`EXECUTABLE_QUOTE_VERIFIED\`: verified time, quantity and actual orderable price/terms; **no such transaction evidence currently supplied**.

Archive timestamps, day, source name, gold purity product, trader ask/bid, FX bank and side, XAU convention. If missing comparable local quotes, Egypt official score is MISSING, no invented premium. Record Egypt FX/financing and local-premium hypotheses in the **secondary macro register**, not copy US gold-factor coefficients into EGP price regression.

Two Egypt shadow baselines to compare prospectively: \`B0_local_ask=last comparable ask\`, and \`F_XAU_FX=latest global gold + same-convention FX*purity*(1+last matched local premium)\`. Price-level accounting and log-error contribution are not causal forecast coefficients. Stage dynamic premium only after first real same-vendor observations are archived and sufficiency assessed.
